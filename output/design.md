# Site Design (Problem 10)

What changed in the Campus Customs website design, and why each change should help shoppers stick around and buy.

## Brand and type

| Change | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Yale blue (`#00356b`) and white throughout, with a navy shield "CC" logo and serif wordmark | Looks like an official Yale shop, which builds trust before someone pays. |
| Soft Yale-blue tinted page background with faint blue glows in the corners (instead of flat gray-white) | Feels livelier and more on-brand, and the white product cards stand out so the merchandise is what catches the eye. |
| Source Serif 4 for headings and Inter for body text, both bundled with the site | Classic collegiate headings with easy-to-read product text. No external font loading, so pages don't flicker or slow down. |

## Shopping (Products page)

| Change | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Left filter sidebar that stays in view while scrolling | Shoppers can narrow down 102 items without scrolling back to the top. Less effort means more browsing. |
| **Multi-select** categories (checkboxes with counts) and **multi-select** color tags with swatches | Lets shoppers compare across types and colors at once (e.g. "hoodies *or* quarter-zips in navy *or* gray") instead of one at a time. |
| Color tags match the **garment color only** (not logo or lettering colors); cards show it ("T-Shirts · heather gray") and the item page lists logo colors separately | Picking "Gray" shows gray shirts, not navy shirts with a gray logo, so results match what shoppers expect and they don't give up on bad matches. |
| Price slider with two handles plus typed min/max | Shows only what fits the budget, so shoppers don't leave after seeing prices they can't afford. |
| Search box, Sort menu (featured, price, name), In stock toggle | Fast paths to a specific item and no dead ends on sold-out products. |
| Removable filter tags, "Clear all", and "Showing X of Y" | Always clear what's being filtered and easy to undo, so shoppers don't get stuck on an empty page. |
| Filters saved in the URL | Opening an item and pressing Back keeps the filtered list, and links can be shared with friends and family. |
| Phone layout: filters slide in as a drawer; 2-column grid; nav wraps to two rows | Most students shop on phones, and nothing is cut off or hard to tap. |

## Product presentation

| Change | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Larger cards: big square photo, name, short tagline (type · colors), price, 2-line description | The product is the hero. Shoppers can judge an item without clicking. |
| "Sold out" badge on cards | Sets expectations before a click, so there's less frustration. |
| Item page: big photo that stays in view, breadcrumb, size tiles showing stock, and "Only N left" in orange | Size availability is clear at a glance, and low-stock signals create urgency to buy now. |

## Favorites (hearts)

| Change | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Heart on every card and on the item page; nav heart with a count opens "Your favorites" | Shoppers can save items while browsing and come back to decide, which keeps them on the site and makes comparing easy. Lists are kept per account (and for guests) in the browser. |

## Motion

| Change | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Cards fade in, lift and zoom on hover; hearts pop; nav underline slides; chat opens smoothly | Makes the site feel polished and responsive, so clicking around is pleasant. |
| All motion turns off for visitors who set "reduce motion" | Comfortable for everyone. |

## Home page

| Change | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Navy hero with real product photos and "Shop the collection" and "Browse hoodies" buttons | Shows actual merchandise right away and gives a one-click path into shopping. |
| "Shop by category" tiles with photos and item counts | Gets shoppers to the right section in one tap. |

## Chat assistant

| Change | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Header with avatar and status dot, rounded bubbles, typing dots | Feels like talking to a real store helper, and shoppers know it's working. |
| Replies show **bold** and bullet lists | Stock and price answers are easy to scan. |
| Three suggested questions in a new chat | Shows what the assistant can do and lowers the effort to ask. |
| "View N items →" button under replies with products | Turns a chat answer straight into browsable product cards. |
| **New chat** button and a **Past chats** list (logged-in shoppers), each header button with a hover label ("Start a new chat", "View past chats", "Close chat") showing each chat's date, time, and first question; date dividers ("Today", "Sat, Sep 19") and a time under every message | Shoppers can start fresh without old questions in the way, or reopen an earlier chat and see when it happened ("the hoodie I asked about last week"), which brings returning customers back to items they were considering. |
